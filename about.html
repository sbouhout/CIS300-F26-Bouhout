Our Story

Welcome to Tresparo Cafe and Bakery! We are passionate about creating a safe and fun space for our community to enjoy our specialty drinks and homemade pastries.

Us three sisters started making custom drinks and pastries for our friends and family, and we realized that we wanted to share our creations with the world. We hope you enjoy our menu as much as we enjoy making it for you!

What We Offer

From classic coffee favorites to refreshing matcha creations, made with ceremonial grade matcha powder, and freshly baked pastries.
